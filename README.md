1. Product mission



SLAMS is a comprehensive acting-training and professional actor-development studio.



It combines structured acting education, practical exercises, scene rehearsal, professional preparation, troubleshooting and guided actor development.



It must feel like a premium creative institution, not a generic educational dashboard.



The product must be useful to:



- Beginners.

- Developing actors.

- Experienced performers.

- Working professionals.

- Actors returning to training.

- Actors preparing for auditions and performances.



2. Immutable architecture



THREE PRIMARY WORLDS:



1. THE CONSERVATORY

   Eight structured acting schools.



2. ACTOR AT WORK

   Auditions, character laboratory, scene breakdown, self-tape studio, scripts, journals and professional preparation.



3. THE STUCK DESK

   Acting problems, practical interventions, exercises and retakes.



SIX SUPPORTING SYSTEMS:



1. Actor's Baseline.

2. Performance Lab.

3. Technique Matrix.

4. Rehearsal Lounge.

5. Actor's Passport / My Actor's Journey.

6. The Director / Master's Chair.



TWO SHARED ENGINES:



1. Living Scene Engine.

2. Actor Development Engine.



FIVE PERMANENT NAVIGATION DESTINATIONS:



OFFICE | SCHOOLS | REHEARSE | WORK | STUCK DESK.



The Office provides four principal actions:



TRAIN | REHEARSE | WORK | GET UNSTUCK.



The ten signature experiences must be integrated into these destinations and contextual workflows. They are not additional navigation destinations.

1. Stanislavski.

6. Laban / Bartenieff.
